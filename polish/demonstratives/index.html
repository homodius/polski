This and thatTen, ta, to · Цей, той

The words for this and that change with gender, number and case, just like Ukrainian цей and той, and their endings are nearly the same: tego · того, temu · тому, tym · тим, tych · тих, tymi · тими. In the tables the part that stays is plain and the ending is coloured. Tap any Polish word to hear it.
