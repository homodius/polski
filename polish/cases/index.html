Polish has seven cases, the same seven as Ukrainian, and they work in much the same way: the ending of a noun, adjective or pronoun changes with its job in the sentence. In every form below the part that stays the same is plain and the ending is coloured; ∅ means no ending. Tap any Polish word to hear it.

First, find the gender

The endings depend on the gender, and masculine nouns split three ways: people, animals and things. In the plural there are only two groups: men, and everything else.
